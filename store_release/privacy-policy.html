1. Information We Collect

Rent & Play collects only the information needed to manage your rental account and equipment bookings, including your name, email address, optional phone number, profile photo, and rental transaction history.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to authenticate your account (including Google Sign-In), process equipment rental requests, verify QR handoffs and returns, and send rental status notifications.

3. Data Storage & Security

Account and rental records are securely stored using industry-standard encrypted cloud services (Firebase Authentication and Supabase). We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

4. Camera & Photo Permissions

The app requests camera or photo gallery access only when scanning equipment/ticket QR codes, uploading optional payment receipts, or updating your profile picture.

5. Account Deletion & Support

You may update your profile details in the app or request account and data deletion at any time by contacting us at rentandplay0@gmail.com.
